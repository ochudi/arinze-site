# How to edit your website

Everything here works on your phone. What you save is on your site a few seconds later.

If you leave a page without saving, the site asks first. Tap **Keep editing** to stay.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open your phone's browser and go to **arinze-nwokolo.com/admin**.
2. Type your email and password.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

You are now on the page called **Your site**. The menu at the top has five words: **Overview**, **Profile**, **Papers**, **Media** and **Writing**.

To look at your site, tap **View the site**. When you finish, tap **Sign out**.

Lost your password? Message Chudi for a new one.

## 2. Change your password

Do this first if Chudi gave you your password.

1. Tap **Overview**.
2. Scroll to the bottom, to **Password**.
3. Under **A new password**, type your new password. Use at least ten characters.
4. Tap **Change password**.
5. Wait for the message **Password changed.**

## 3. Edit your profile text

1. Tap **Profile**.
2. Find the box you want:
   - **Title** is the job title under your name.
   - **About you** is the paragraph on your front page.
   - **Email** is the address on your front page and at the foot of every page.
3. Change the words.
4. Tap **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen.
5. Wait for the message **Saved. Your profile is on the site.**

Leave **Opening sentence** empty. If you fill it in, that sentence appears in large type above your paragraph.

## 4. Add a paper by pasting a DOI

1. Tap **Papers**.
2. Tap **Add a paper**.
3. Paste the DOI into the box **Already published? Paste its DOI**.
4. Tap **Fill in the details**. The form fills itself in.
5. Read through the form. Check the title, year and co-authors. The year is the year of the printed issue, which can be later than the year the paper appeared online.
6. To show the paper on your front page, tick **Show on the front page**. The front page shows the three newest papers ticked.
7. Tap **Add this paper**.

If you see **No paper found for this DOI**, check the DOI. Or fill in the form yourself.

## 5. Move a paper to another section

1. Tap **Papers**.
2. Tap the paper.
3. Under **Section**, choose **Publications**, **Working papers** or **Work in progress**.
4. If the paper is now published, also do these:
   - Change **Year** to the year of publication.
   - Under **Journal or series**, type the journal's name.
   - Under **Volume, issue, pages**, type the details, like this: 68(3), 942–957.
   - Empty the **Status** box.
   - Under **Link to the paper**, paste the journal's web address.
5. Tap **Save changes**.

The paper's title opens its PDF if it has one. If there is no PDF, the title opens the link. To send readers to the journal instead, tap **Remove** beside the PDF.

To keep the old working paper too, go to **More links** and tap **Add a link**. Type "Working paper version", then paste its web address.

## 6. Upload a new CV

1. Have the new CV on your phone as a PDF. It must be under 20 MB.
2. Tap **Profile**.
3. Scroll to **Contact**, then to **CV**.
4. Tap **Replace the PDF**.
5. Choose the file.
6. Wait for the message **Uploaded. Save to put it on the site.**
7. Tap **Save changes**.

The CV link in your menu now opens the new file. The old file is removed. Links to your CV keep working.

## 7. Write and publish an essay

To write it:

1. Tap **Writing**.
2. Tap **Add a piece**.
3. Fill in **Title**, **Date** and **Summary**. The summary is one or two sentences. It appears under the title and when the piece is shared.
4. Tick **Keep as a draft**. A draft is saved but stays off your site.
5. Leave **Outlet** and **Link to the piece** empty.
6. Write under **Text**. To format words, select them and tap a button above the text.
7. Tap **Add this piece**.
8. Wait for the message **Saved as a draft. It is not on the site yet.**

To come back to it, tap **Writing**, then tap the piece. Tap **Save changes** when you finish.

To publish it:

1. Tap **Writing**.
2. Tap the piece.
3. Untick **Keep as a draft**.
4. Tap **Save changes**.
5. The message now says the piece is on the site.

Your first published piece adds **Writing** to the menu on your site.

If the piece appeared somewhere else, such as a newspaper, you do not need to paste the text. Fill in **Outlet** and **Link to the piece**, and leave **Text** empty. The title then opens that page.

## 8. Read the Readers counts

1. Tap **Overview**.
2. Scroll to **Readers**.
3. Each paper has two numbers: **Last 30 days** and **All time**. The most-read paper is at the top.

A paper is counted when someone opens it from its title on your site. The counts are close, not exact. They tell you nothing about who the visitors are.

If no paper has been opened yet, you see a short note instead of the list.

Anything beyond this, message Chudi.
